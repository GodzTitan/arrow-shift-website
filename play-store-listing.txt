FULL DESCRIPTION
Welcome to Arrow Shift — a relaxing but challenging arrow puzzle game where every tap matters.

Guide arrows off the board in the right order, avoid blocked moves, and conquer 500 levels of increasing difficulty. From small starter grids to massive boss and god-tier challenges, Arrow Shift keeps your brain engaged with clean visuals and smooth one-finger controls.

FEATURES
• 500 puzzle levels
• Boss and god challenge tiers
• 3 lives on every fresh level
• Hint system for tough boards
• In-game shop with arrow styles
• Light and dark theme
• Resume mid-level progress
• Pinch to zoom on large grids
• Offline-friendly local save

How to play:
Tap an arrow to slide it out of the grid. If its path is blocked, you lose a life. Clear every arrow to complete the level and earn stars.
