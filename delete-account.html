What Data Is Deleted?

When your account is deleted through this portal:

  • Account ID: User UID and Firebase authentication session.
  • Cloud Gameplay Data: Highest level completed, stars earned, current lives, and hints.
  • Virtual Coins & Unlocks: Coin balances, purchased arrow cosmetics, and inventory.
  • Saved Settings: Sound, music, vibration, and theme configurations.

What Data Is Retained?

None. No gameplay data, profile identifiers, or analytics linking to your UID are retained once deleted.
