Account & Data Deletion Request

Effective date: October 2026

Alien X Software is committed to respecting your privacy and giving you full control over your data. In compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion and Data Safety policies, this page provides clear instructions on how you can request the permanent deletion of your Arrow Shift account and all associated cloud data.

App Information:

• App Name: Arrow Shift

• Package Name: com.alienx.arrowshift

• Developer: Alien X Software

• Support Email: support@alienxsoftware.com

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can request account and data deletion through either of the following methods:

Method 1: Email Request (Web / External)

You can submit a deletion request via email directly to our support team:

  1. Open Arrow Shift on your device and navigate to Settings.
  2. Under Cloud & Profile, locate your User UID and copy it.
  3. Send an email to support@alienxsoftware.com with:
    • Subject: Arrow Shift Account and Data Deletion Request
    • Body: Include your User UID and a brief statement requesting deletion of your account and game data.

Method 2: In-App Reset & Deletion

If you have the app installed, you can also delete your local data immediately:

  • Open Arrow Shift → tap the Settings icon.
  • Tap Delete Account & Data under the Cloud & Profile section.
  • Confirm the prompt. This clears your local game progress, coin balances, unlocked themes, and revokes local tokens.
  • Uninstalling the app will also immediately remove all locally cached gameplay data from your device.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When an account and data deletion request is processed, we permanently delete:

  • Account Identifier: Your Firebase Cloud Authentication UID and associated session tokens.
  • Game Progress Data: Highest level unlocked, level completion stars, and mid-level progress.
  • Virtual Currency & Items: Coin balances, hints, lives, life refills, and cosmetic arrow shop unlocks.
  • Game Preferences: Saved audio, haptic, and theme settings.

What Data Is Retained?

None. We do not retain any personal data or gameplay records after your deletion request is completed. If required by applicable financial laws, anonymized transactional logs for in-app purchases processed through Google Play may be maintained by Google Play according to Google's standard retention schedules.

Deletion Timeframe

• In-App / Device Deletion: Immediate upon selecting delete or uninstalling the app.
• Cloud / Server Deletion: Requests sent to support@alienxsoftware.com are processed and permanently purged from our Firebase Firestore database within 30 days (typically within 48 to 72 hours).

Need Help?

If you have any questions regarding your data or our deletion process, please contact us at support@alienxsoftware.com.
